---
name: flowgate-issue
description: Prepare, split or publish a scoped FlowGate lab task, bug or design issue using the canonical issue forms.
---

Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md), Issues and acceptance. Select
the matching form in [ISSUE_TEMPLATE](../../../.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE) and keep
its field definitions authoritative; do not maintain a separate format here.

- Use a task for an independently verifiable deliverable, a bug for a concrete
  reproduced failure, or a design for a decision/controlled change.
- Identify outcome, boundaries, acceptance, verification phases, human owner,
  dependencies and risk/recovery. Distinguish supplied evidence from assumptions.
- Split a broad result into a parent and independently useful subissues. Multiple
  PR stages must state their individual delivery and outstanding acceptance.
- Record proposed versus approved design status and the exact approved scope/version.
  Material changes need renewed approval; publication is not implementation approval.

Prepare a reviewable body before authorized publication. The coordinator publishes
unless remote issue actions were specifically delegated. Do not invent URLs, issue
numbers, owners, approval or observed failure. For authorized non-delivery closure,
record reason and replacement and never mark Done.

Return the prepared artifact or actual issue link, readiness, missing evidence and
next action. Ordinary Refs and closing associations follow CONTRIBUTING.md.
